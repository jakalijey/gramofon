# Gramofon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Gramofon is a music app made by Heylogram. In short: you have no account with us, there are no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Your payment is a public Bitcoin Cash transaction.

## Your wallet

You connect a Bitcoin Cash wallet of your choice, for example Paytaca or Selene, with WizardConnect. The page and your wallet talk through encrypted relays run by Riften Labs (relay.riften.net and relay.cauldron.quest). Your wallet shows every transaction before you sign it. We never see your recovery words or private keys, and we cannot move your funds.

## Your payment

A membership is paid with a normal Bitcoin Cash transaction. Like every transaction on Bitcoin Cash, it is public and permanent. To give you your membership and to make sure a payment is used only once, our server checks the transaction with public Electrum servers and keeps a short record: the transaction id, your wallet address, the amount, the time and the date your membership ends. The Bitcoin Cash price used for the quote comes from Kraken and Coinbase; our server asks them, not your browser. The upload fee for a song works the same way; its record also notes which song the fee was used for.

## If you upload a song

When you hand a song to the man in the recording room, we receive the song file, its original file name, the cover picture if you add one, and the song name and artist name you type. They are stored in our private storage with Contabo in Europe so we can listen to the song.

Your email address is optional. We ask for it only if you tick "Email me a receipt and the review result", and use it for two emails about your song: a receipt when it arrives, and the result once it's reviewed. We delete it once the result email is sent, unless you also ticked "Also email me a monthly report of my plays and earnings": then, if your song is approved, we keep your address to send you that report once a month while your song is on Gramofon. Reply "stop" to any report and we delete it. You can also give us this address later, change it or remove it under "My songs and earnings" in the recording room; then we keep it only for the monthly report. All these emails come from gramofon@heylogram.com.

Your earnings are paid in Bitcoin Cash to the wallet address you paid the upload fee from, which we already keep with the payment record. "My songs and earnings" in the recording room shows your plays, listeners, share, balance and payouts. It uses the same wallet sign-in as members: your wallet shares its public keys with this page (never its private keys, and nothing is paid or signed), our server uses them once to find the upload fees paid from that wallet, and we do not keep those keys.

When you hand in a song, our server makes an audio fingerprint of it: a short code that describes the sound, not the sound itself. We compare it with the fingerprints of songs already on Gramofon or handed in before. We keep the fingerprint even if the song is declined (the audio file itself is deleted), so the same recording is recognized if it's handed in again.

To check whether a song has already been released, we also look up the song name and the artist name in MusicBrainz, a public music database. If it has, we ask you for proof that it's yours before the song goes live.

If we decline a song you made yourself, the upload fee goes back to the wallet address you paid from, which we already keep with the payment record.

The board in the entrance hall shows the song name, the artist name and the review status to every visitor: songs under review, approved songs for two weeks and songs that were not approved for one week. If a song is not approved, its file and cover are deleted from our storage. An approved song is added to the gramophone's playlist.

## Plays

Half of every membership goes to the artists you play, so we count plays. When you have listened to an uploaded song for 30 seconds, your browser tells our server, and we record the song, the time and the wallet address your membership was paid from. We use these records only to share membership money between artists, to send artists their monthly numbers (how many plays and how many listeners, never who), and to spot plays that look automated, which are not counted. When an artist plays their own song, the play counts and their report shows how many of the plays were their own. We keep them for 13 months.

## Radio

The gramophone composes its radio songs in your browser; no recording is sent to us. When you send a radio song to the gramophone, we keep the song's number, the time, and a one-way code made from your wallet address, so that each member can send at most five songs a day. We never show who sent a song. This list is kept on our server, with a backup copy in our private storage with Contabo in Europe.

## What stays in your browser

Gramofon sets no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only for what the app needs to work, which is why we don't ask for cookie consent; a short notice tells you about it on your first visit.

Your listening ticket, the end date of your membership and an unfinished payment (if any) are kept in your browser's local storage, together with small settings such as whether the playlist is open. If you paid an upload fee, your upload pass stays there until your song is sent, and the songs you uploaded from this browser are remembered so the hall can show you their status. They stay on your device. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, you can sign in with the wallet you paid with: your wallet shares its public keys with this page (never its private keys, and nothing is paid or signed), and our server uses them once to find your payment and give your membership back. We do not keep those keys.

## Music and covers

Songs and covers come from api.heylogram.com. To play a song, your browser sends your listening ticket with the request. The songs themselves are stored with Contabo in Europe; no listener data is stored there.

## Our servers

gramofon.heylogram.com and api.heylogram.com are served from Heylogram's server. We do not keep request logs for Gramofon. Technical error logs may hold an IP address for a short time. Payment and upload records are kept on that server, with a backup copy in our private storage with Contabo in Europe.

## Children

Gramofon is not meant for children under 13.
