# Gramofon Terms of Use

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Gramofon is a music app made by Heylogram. By using it you accept these terms.

## Membership

- A membership lets you play every song on Gramofon for 30 days. It is kept in the browser where you paid; on another device or after clearing your browser, sign in with the wallet you paid with to get it back.
- The price is shown in US dollars and paid in Bitcoin Cash at the rate quoted when you pay. A quote is valid for 10 minutes.
- Your membership starts when the payment is seen on the Bitcoin Cash network.

## Radio

- The gramophone composes songs itself, and you hear new ones on every visit in the Radio tab. Listening needs a membership, like every song on Gramofon.
- If you like a radio song, you can send it to the gramophone for free, and it joins everyone's playlist. There's no fee for sending, and nobody is paid for these songs. A member can send up to five songs a day.
- A song you send gets a made-up title, a made-up artist name and a drawn cover, chosen by the gramophone. These artists are not real people, and the playlist marks their songs "from the radio".

## Uploading a song

- Uploading costs a small fee, shown in US dollars and paid in Bitcoin Cash. It covers the cost of reviewing and storing your song. One fee is for one song; if you pay and don't upload right away, your upload pass stays valid for 30 days in the browser where you paid.
- Upload only music you made yourself or have the rights to share. Uploading someone else's music is not allowed.
- Every song is checked automatically the moment you hand it in. We compare its sound with every song already on Gramofon or handed in before, even if it was renamed, cut, sped up or pitch-shifted, and we look up released songs in public music databases.
- If the sound matches a song that belongs to someone else, the song is not accepted and the fee is not refunded.
- If the song has already been released, we need proof that it's yours before it goes on the gramophone. Proof is something we can check: a short code we give you, posted on the artist's official account or website; an email from the artist's management, label or distributor; or the release on your verified artist profile. Without proof, the song is not accepted and the fee is not refunded.
- Every song that passes the check is listened to by hand, and we may approve or decline any song. If we decline a song you made yourself (for example, it isn't a fit for Gramofon), we send the fee back to the wallet you paid from.
- You keep all rights to your music. By uploading, you let Gramofon store your song and play it to Gramofon members. You can ask us to take your song down at any time: gramofon@heylogram.com.

## Artist earnings

- Half of every membership goes to the artists that member plays, split by plays. A play counts after a member listens to a song for 30 seconds. An artist who is also a member counts like any other member, so their plays of their own songs count too. Plays that look automated are not counted.
- Earnings are added up once a month and paid in Bitcoin Cash to the wallet you paid the upload fee from. If your balance is below $1, it carries over to the next month.
- You can see your plays, listeners, share, balance and payouts at any time: in the recording room, tap "My songs and earnings". In the browser you uploaded from it opens straight away; on another device, connect the wallet you paid the upload fee with.
- We email you a monthly report with the same numbers if you asked for it when you uploaded, or turned it on later under "My songs and earnings". You can turn it off there, or by replying "stop" to a report.

## Payments

Payments are made on the Bitcoin Cash blockchain and cannot be reversed by us. If you paid and did not get your membership or your upload pass, write to us with the transaction id and we will sort it out.

## Music

Songs are uploaded by their artists, and every song is reviewed by hand before it goes on the gramophone. Artists keep all rights to their music. You may listen for your own personal use; downloading, recording or sharing the songs is not allowed.
